# Privacy policy

  Cloud Backup runs on your own Home Assistant.

  - It keeps copies of your Home Assistant backups in your own Google Drive, and can read or delete them there.
  - It can only see files it created itself.
  - Your Google sign-in is kept only on your Home Assistant.
  - Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. No tracking.

  You can remove its access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
